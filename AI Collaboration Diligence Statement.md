# AI Collaboration Diligence Statement

> *A reflection on the responsible use of artificial intelligence in the development of custom-de*

---

## Overview

The Security & Advocacy Program acknowledges the use of artificial intelligence as a collaborative tool in the creation, structuring, and communication of its program materials. This statement is offered in the interest of transparency and professional integrity — affirming that AI collaboration within this program is governed by deliberate standards of care across three distinct dimensions: **Creation Diligence**, **Transparency Diligence**, and **Deployment Diligence**.

This document does not serve as a disclaimer. It serves as a commitment — a reflection of how this program approaches the integration of AI-assisted work with the same rigor applied to all other aspects of its security and compliance philosophy.

---

## Creation Diligence

Creation Diligence refers to the standards applied when AI is used to generate, draft, or structure program content.

All AI-assisted content produced within the scope of this program is subject to the following practices:

- **Human oversight is required at every stage.** No AI-generated content is treated as final or authoritative without review, validation, and approval by a qualified human contributor.
- **Accuracy is verified against source material.** AI outputs are cross-referenced with established compliance frameworks, security standards, and program-specific objectives to ensure factual integrity.
- **Content is shaped by domain expertise.** AI is used to accelerate drafting and organization — not to substitute for the subject matter knowledge, professional judgment, or lived experience of the program's contributors.
- **Iterative refinement is the standard.** Outputs are reviewed across multiple passes to ensure alignment with the program's goals, tone, and audience expectations.

The use of AI in content creation is understood to be a tool that amplifies human effort, not one that replaces human responsibility.

---

## Transparency Diligence

Transparency Diligence refers to the obligation to openly acknowledge AI's role in the program's work and to ensure that all stakeholders have an accurate understanding of how content was produced.

This program is committed to the following transparency practices:

- **AI involvement is disclosed proactively.** Where AI has meaningfully contributed to the creation or structuring of program materials, that contribution is acknowledged — as demonstrated by the existence of this statement.
- **Stakeholders are not misled about authorship.** Program materials represent a collaboration between human contributors and AI tools. The program does not attribute AI-assisted work solely to human authorship, nor does it obscure the nature of that collaboration.
- **The limitations of AI are recognized and communicated.** AI systems can produce plausible but incorrect, incomplete, or contextually inappropriate content. This program treats such limitations as a known risk that must be actively managed through review and validation.
- **This statement is maintained as a living document.** As AI capabilities and the program's use of them evolve, this statement will be updated to reflect current practices accurately.

Transparency in AI collaboration is not optional for a program built on trust, compliance, and security advocacy. It is foundational.

---

## Deployment Diligence

Deployment Diligence refers to the standards applied when AI-assisted content is published, distributed, or acted upon — ensuring that what reaches stakeholders has been responsibly validated and is fit for its intended purpose.

The following practices govern the deployment of AI-assisted program materials:

- **Review gates precede publication.** AI-assisted content does not reach external stakeholders without passing through a structured review process that evaluates accuracy, appropriateness, and alignment with program objectives.
- **Content is contextually appropriate.** Materials are evaluated not only for factual accuracy but for tone, cultural sensitivity, and relevance to the specific audiences and industries they are intended to serve.
- **Version control and change tracking are maintained.** Revisions to AI-assisted materials are documented to ensure accountability and to support future audits of how content has evolved over time.
- **Feedback loops are in place.** Stakeholders who identify errors, gaps, or concerns in deployed content are encouraged to raise them. Reported issues are reviewed promptly and addressed with the same rigor applied to all program deliverables.
- **AI-assisted outputs are never deployed in contexts that require certified expertise without independent validation.** Where compliance decisions, legal interpretations, or security control recommendations are concerned, AI-generated content serves only as a starting point — not as a definitive or authoritative source.

---

## Closing Reflection

The Security & Advocacy Program exists to close the gap between compliance intent and real-world implementation. That mission demands intellectual honesty — including honesty about the tools used to advance it.

AI collaboration, when practiced with rigor and transparency, can strengthen the program's capacity to communicate clearly, structure information effectively, and engage stakeholders at scale. This statement reflects a commitment to ensuring that such collaboration never comes at the expense of the trust, accuracy, and professional integrity upon which this program is built.
